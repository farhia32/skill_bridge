# SkillBridge – Connecting Talent to Opportunities

### Your Future Starts With the Right Opportunity.

## 1. Project Overview

SkillBridge is a web-based platform designed to connect students, graduates, and young professionals with valuable opportunities for personal, academic, and career development.

Finding scholarships, internships, and quality learning resources can be challenging because information is scattered across different websites and platforms.

SkillBridge provides a centralized platform where users can discover educational and career opportunities, explore free online learning resources, and take steps towards achieving their goals.

## 2. Problem Statement

Many students and young graduates struggle to find reliable scholarships, internship opportunities, and accessible learning resources.

Information is often distributed across multiple websites, making the search process time-consuming and overwhelming.

As a result, individuals may miss valuable opportunities that could support their education, professional development, and career growth.

## 3. Proposed Solution

SkillBridge addresses this challenge by bringing different educational and career opportunities together in one accessible platform.

Through its organized interface and interactive features, users can explore opportunities, search and filter available listings, discover online courses, and manage their learning journey.

The platform aims to make opportunity discovery easier, more organized, and accessible.

## 4. Project Objectives

The main objectives of SkillBridge are:

- To provide a centralized platform for discovering scholarships and internships.
- To simplify the process of searching for educational and career opportunities.
- To provide access to free online learning resources.
- To help users identify opportunities that match their interests.
- To create a user-friendly platform that supports academic and professional growth.

## 5. Key Features

### 1. Opportunities Page
- Displays available scholarships and internships.

- Provides category and location filtering.
- Displays relevant opportunity information.

### 2. Learning Hub
- Provides access to online courses and learning resources.
- Organizes courses into different categories.
- Allows users to filter courses based on their interests.
- Connects users to external learning platforms.

### 3. User Dashboard
- Provides a central space for users to explore their activities and opportunities.

### 4. User Authentication Pages
- Login page.
- Sign-up page.
- Registration success/confirmation page.

### 5. Opportunity Details
- Provides additional information about selected opportunities.

### 6. Responsive Navigation
- Provides navigation between the different sections of the website.
- Makes it easier for users to access the platform's features.

2. Open the project folder:

3. Open the `index.html` file in your web browser.
